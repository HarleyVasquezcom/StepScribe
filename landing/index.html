StepScribe

Do it once, print the steps. StepScribe watches your clicks and typed values on a page and turns them into a numbered instruction sheet with mini captures — exported as Markdown or HTML.

Download the extension (.zip)

Interactions in

Clicks become click #button steps; each field change records the target selector and the typed value. Sequence numbers stamp every step.

Mini element captures

When the clicked element is an image, canvas or video, its region is rasterized with drawImage into a tiny thumbnail embedded in the sheet.

Export the sheet

Download Markdown or a self-contained HTML page with the numbered instructions — thumbnails included. Nothing leaves your machine.

Install (load unpacked)

  1. Download stepscribe.zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the folder.
  4. Open a page, start recording from the popup, work through your flow, export.
